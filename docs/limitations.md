# Limitations

1. **Toy models.** Islands, the unitary/non-unitary evaporation, and the horizon pair models are finite-dimensional or phenomenological. They illustrate structure; they do not derive it from quantum gravity.
2. **Hawking radiation.** The thermal spectrum is imposed from $T_H$; the Bogoliubov derivation is not implemented. The pair-state model is a caricature of it.
3. **Greybody factors** are for a scalar field (spin-1 and spin-2 potentials are plotted but not used for emission rates) and a Schwarzschild background only.
4. **Evaporation** is photon-only blackbody, with no species counting and no endpoint physics. The adiabaticity parameter $\varepsilon$ is heuristic and, in this model, does not signal a breakdown before the Planck scale.
5. **Log-correction coefficients** are free parameters; none is endorsed.
6. **Page curve** results are averages over Haar-random states; they say nothing about dynamics.
7. **Replica method**: only the finite-dimensional identity is tested; no gravitational replica geometry.
8. **Holography**: static AdS$_3$/BTZ geodesics only.
9. **Lattice**: a one-dimensional free scalar; infrared mass effects reduce the fitted slope below $c/3$ for the default parameters (0.297).
10. **Scale.** Exact state-vector methods limit register sizes to $\sim14$ qubits.
11. **Bibliography** was compiled from memory in an environment without access to arXiv, Crossref or INSPIRE and has not been checked entry by entry; see [references.md](references.md).
12. **Constants** were entered by hand, not fetched from a database.
13. **Not claimed:** resolution of the information paradox, a derivation of black-hole microstates, or any new physical result.
